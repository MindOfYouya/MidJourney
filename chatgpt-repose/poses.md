# Pose blocks

Replace only the `POSE (follow exactly)` section at the end of `master-prompt.txt` (from the `POSE` line to the end of the prompt). Leave everything above it untouched.

Rule of thumb: leg-driven poses (walk, crouch, sit) need a bigger turn of about 50-60 degrees, or the legs get foreshortened. Arm-driven poses can stay near 30 degrees.

## Slot template

```
POSE (follow exactly)
[NAME].
View: [front-three-quarter, TURN IN DEGREES toward the viewer's left, chest still visible; head turn so both ears show].
Legs: [WHICH LEG IS FORWARD OR CARRIES THE WEIGHT; HEEL OR TOE; both boots fully visible].
Torso and head: [LEAN IN DEGREES; CHIN; GAZE; BROWS AND MOUTH; nose and both ears visible].
His right arm (viewer's left): [ELBOW; HAND POSITION; WHAT IT HOLDS; clear of the pouch].
His left arm (viewer's right): [ELBOW; HAND POSITION; WHAT IT HOLDS OR TOUCHES].
Flask: [WHERE; UPRIGHT OR TILTED; cork direction; fully visible].
Cloth and bags: [COAT TAIL DIRECTION; WHERE SATCHEL AND POUCH HANG].
Ground: [SHADOW; NONE OR ONE PLAIN PROP].
```

## Tired road walk (default in master-prompt.txt)

Medium difficulty. Keeps the reference's facing, so every costume side maps one-to-one.

```
POSE (follow exactly)
Tired road walk, mid-stride.
View: front-three-quarter, turned about 55 degrees toward the viewer's left, chest still visible, walking that way; head turned about 30 degrees back toward the camera so both ears and the amber eyes stay visible.
Legs: viewer's-left leg forward, heel down, toe up; viewer's-right leg trailing, heel lifted; both boots fully visible.
Torso and head: leans forward about 15 degrees, shoulders dropped; chin lowered about 10 degrees, heavy-lidded amber eyes on the road, one brow raised; nose and both ears clearly visible.
His right arm (viewer's left): elbow slightly bent, swinging forward and away from the body, hand holding the flask upright by its body at waist height, clear of the pouch.
His left arm (viewer's right): hand gripping the strap where it crosses his waist just above the satchel flap, off the pouch.
Flask: upright, cork up, fully visible, not overlapping the pouch.
Cloth and bags: coat tails sway back toward the viewer's right, frayed hem visible; satchel swings gently.
Ground: soft shadow under both boots.
```

## Heroic stand, presenting the flask

Easy to draw, but the highest risk of drifting back to the reference pose.

```
POSE (follow exactly)
Heroic stand, presenting the flask.
View: front-three-quarter, turned about 30 degrees toward the viewer's left.
Legs: wide planted stance, boots about two boot-lengths apart, weight on the viewer's-right leg with the knee slightly bent, viewer's-left leg straight, toe outward; both boots fully visible.
Torso and head: stands tall, chest out, shoulders back; chin level, brow lifted, smug half-smile; both ears flared.
His right arm (viewer's left): extended out to the side at arm's length, hand holding the flask by its body at shoulder height, at least one head-width away from his face.
His left arm (viewer's right): fist on the hip above the satchel, elbow out, off the pouch.
Flask: upright, cork up, fully visible, same size as in the reference.
Cloth and bags: coat tails lift slightly and drift back toward the viewer's right, clear of the hip; satchel and pouch hang still.
Ground: shadow centred under the stance.
```

## Guarding his stock, hunched and wary

Hard. Turning the body risks swapped sides or a flask hidden by the arm. If it fails, reduce the turn to about 20 degrees.

```
POSE (follow exactly)
Guarding his stock, hunched and wary.
View: front-three-quarter, body turned about 45 degrees toward the viewer's left so his left side and satchel face the camera; coat front still visible, no back view; head turned toward the viewer.
Legs: knees bent about 30 degrees, weight on the viewer's-right leg with the boot flat; viewer's-left leg stepping away, toe down, heel lifted.
Torso and head: curled forward about 30 degrees around the flask, shoulders raised; chin lowered, narrowed amber eyes, lips pressed tight; both ears flared.
His right arm (viewer's left): elbow tucked at the ribs, hand cradling the flask's base at breastbone height.
His left arm (viewer's right): forearm wrapped across the chest over the flask's shoulder, fingers curled over the glass.
Flask: pressed upright against the chest, with the corked neck and glowing yellow-green body still visible.
Cloth and bags: coat hem lifts slightly toward the viewer's right; satchel pressed against his left hip, strap taut; pouch stays at the front of the belt below the crossed forearm.
Ground: compact shadow under both boots.
```

## Seated on a stool, taking a break

Hard. The stool may gain decoration, and the coat may swallow the legs or rearrange the bags.

```
POSE (follow exactly)
Seated on a plain stool, taking a break.
View: front-three-quarter, turned about 60 degrees toward the viewer's left so thighs and shins read as clear horizontal and vertical shapes; whole stool in frame.
Legs: sits on the front half of a plain three-legged wooden stool, thighs roughly horizontal, knees bent about 90 degrees; viewer's-left boot flat ahead, viewer's-right boot tucked back toe-down; both boots fully visible.
Torso and head: slumped, leaning forward about 20 degrees, shoulders dropped; chin lowered, heavy-lidded amber eyes, lips relaxed; both ears visible.
His right arm (viewer's left): forearm along the viewer's-left thigh, hand holding the flask upright on the knee.
His left arm (viewer's right): forearm across the viewer's-right thigh, hand open and loose, palm up, clear of both bags.
Flask: upright on the knee, cork up, fully visible.
Cloth and bags: coat skirt drapes behind the stool; satchel hangs at the viewer's-right hip, strap slack; pouch stays at the front of the belt.
Ground: one plain stool only; shadow under the stool and boots.
```
